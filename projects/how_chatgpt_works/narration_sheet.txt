NARRATION SHEET — How ChatGPT works (series intro / episode 1)
Total target: 75s
Record one take per section; keep each within its time box.

[  0s –   9s]  (hook)
  ChatGPT has no idea what it's saying. Seriously. It's doing one thing, over and over: guessing the next word. Here's the whole trick, in one minute.

[  9s –  25s]  (tokens)
  Step one: your message gets chopped into tokens. Not words — chunks. Watch: chatgpt becomes two tokens. And every token is turned into a long list of numbers, because neural networks only speak math.

[ 25s –  40s]  (transformer)
  Step two: those numbers flow up through a transformer — a giant stack of layers where every token looks at every other token and asks: who matters for what comes next? That's the famous attention mechanism.

[ 40s –  57s]  (predict)
  Step three: out comes a probability for every possible next token. The capital of France is... Paris, ninety-two percent. It picks one, sticks it on the end, and runs the whole loop again. That's it. And notice — it ranks likely words. It never checks facts. That's exactly why it can be confidently wrong.

[ 57s –  70s]  (roadmap)
  So that was the ten-thousand-foot view. Coming up in this series: tokenizers — why AI can't spell. Embeddings — how meaning becomes math. And attention — the hundred-billion-dollar idea.
