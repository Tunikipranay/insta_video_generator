  swap() cross-fades the old beat out as the new one fades in, and centres
  and scales the new group for you. It is the ONLY way to change content:
  never leave a previous beat's shapes on screen, and never FadeOut
  everything with nothing replacing it. If you animate an extra element
  into the current beat, add it to the group (cur.add(x)) so the next swap
  removes it too. The title stays up the whole scene.
